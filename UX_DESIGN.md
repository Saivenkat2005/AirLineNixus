# AIRLINE NEXUS — SPATIAL DESIGN SYSTEM & UX SPECIFICATIONS

## 1. Design Philosophy
- **Aesthetic**: Cinematic Aviation + Spatial Glassmorphism.
- **Color Palette**: Deep Midnight Navy (`#050A14`, `#08111F`), Aviation Cyan (`#38BDF8`), Electric Blue (`#0284C7`), Emerald Success (`#34D399`), and Amber Warning (`#FBBF24`).
- **Typography**: Inter / Outfit / Geist with crisp hierarchy and legible monospace identifiers for flights and PNRs.

---

## 2. Micro-Interactions & Motion
- **Restrained Motion**: 200–400ms cubic-bezier transitions for card lifts, detail drawers, and modal overlays.
- **Accessibility**: Strict adherence to `prefers-reduced-motion` and minimum 4.5:1 WCAG contrast ratios.

---

## 3. Theming & PWA
- **Themes Supported**: `Airline Dark`, `Airline Light`, `AMOLED`, `Midnight Blue`.
- **PWA Ready**: Offline caching banner, service worker integration, installable web manifest.
